> ⚠️ **Pre-release.** Not feature-complete, bugs are expected. This is a preview
> for testing, not a finished tool.

v0.2 is about **trustworthy monitoring**: every number now says what it really is,
more battery and GPU data is shown where your device actually reports it, and the
Home screen is easier to read at a glance.

## Highlights

- **Honest CPU/GPU percentages.** The old "CPU %" and "GPU %" were never
  utilisation — they show how close the clock is to its maximum. They are now
  labelled **Clock %** (Home) and **CPU clock % / GPU clock %** (overlay).
- **Real GPU load** where the Adreno driver exposes it. On devices that don't,
  the row is hidden instead of showing a guess.
- **Better GPU graph.** Pick **30 s / 1 min / 5 min**, with now / average / peak
  readouts.
- **More battery detail:** charge state and charger type, voltage, and power
  (voltage × current), each shown only when your device reports it.
- **Correct CPU cluster layout** on chips where two clusters share a max clock
  (e.g. `3+2+2+1` instead of `3+4+1`).
- **Cleaner Home screen.** GPU, CPU, memory & battery and performance each have
  one card. GPU Lock is now a compact control inside the GPU card.
- **Data-driven RAM cleaner** (needs Shizuku). See how much memory each app
  holds and how low your RAM usage can realistically go. Pick apps to stop and
  block from the background, then compare the **measured** result with the
  estimate. **Restore** puts every blocked app back exactly as it was.
- **Floating overlay:** with many metrics enabled, the horizontal bar wraps onto
  up to three lines instead of shrinking until it's unreadable and still clipped.

## Upgrading from v0.1

Install over v0.1; settings and recorded sessions are kept. Your overlay metric
choices carry over. "CPU %" / "GPU %" simply appear under their new names.

## Worth knowing before you try it

- **There is still no real CPU utilisation reading.** It needs root on modern
  Android. **Clock %** is a clock ratio, not how busy the CPU is.
- **Battery current and power depend on the device.** Some phones report current
  in the wrong unit, so these can read wrong. VZtats doesn't guess a correction.
- **Apps blocked by the RAM cleaner don't send notifications** until you restore
  them, and a target like 30% may not be reachable. The cleaner shows the realistic
  floor for your device instead of promising a number.
- **GPU Lock is Adreno-only** and some kernels ignore it. The app tells you when
  the request wasn't verified. Locking **increases heat and battery drain**. ⚠️

## Feedback

Most useful right now: what the **RAM cleaner** reports as the lowest reachable
usage on your device, whether the **GPU Load** row appears, whether
the **CPU cluster label** matches your chipset, and whether **battery current,
voltage and power** look plausible while charging and on battery. Please include
your device model, Android version and chipset when
[opening an issue](https://github.com/Vauzi-17/vztats/issues).
